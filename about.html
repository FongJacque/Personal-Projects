About Me

I'm a Statistics and Data Science student at UCLA, and I took the long way here. Before going back to school, I spent nine years in the U.S. Army as an intelligence analyst, trained at Le Cordon Bleu, and supervised security operations at a client site in Santa Clara.

The common thread has been data. In the Army I built datasets from many sources and turned them into clear, time-sensitive answers. As a security supervisor I audited daily reports and incident logs, and as a data science intern I prepared data for recommendation models. Studying statistics is how I'm learning the theory behind work I'd already been doing for years.

These days I work mostly in R and Python, with some C++, and I'm especially interested in statistical modeling, machine learning, and computer vision.
