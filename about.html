About Me

I'm a Statistics and Data Science student at UCLA, and I took the long way here: nine years as an intelligence analyst in the U.S. Army, classical French training at Le Cordon Bleu, and supervising security operations in Santa Clara before going back to school.

Through all of it, what matters most to me is people: being someone others can rely on, and helping the people around me succeed.

That mix of experiences taught me to look at a problem from many angles. It's also what I love about data. The same numbers can tell several stories, and the real work is seeing how each one fits into the bigger picture.

What I'm working on

Right now I'm focused on R projects, since R is at the heart of statistics, while keeping up with Python and computer vision. My favorite classes have been the Python ones: I love the logical structure of programming, and that it's a skill you can measurably grow.

The project I'm proudest of is my image segmentation program. Building it from scratch showed me how computers recognize images, and how much complexity sits beneath programs that look simple on the surface.

Long term, I want to use data to make things better for people, whether that's cancer research, autonomous driving, or making everyday life a little more efficient. I haven't settled on one industry yet, and I'd like to experience several.
